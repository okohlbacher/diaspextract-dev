`git ls-tree` blob id and mode, against dev 6c0adce's tracked files minus `.export-private`. The two sets match:

- dev's four `assets/*.svg` (`algorithm`, `logo`, `pipeline`, `streaming`) have the same blobs in public;
- public additionally keeps its two public-only files `assets/logo.png` and `assets/logo-128.png`.
