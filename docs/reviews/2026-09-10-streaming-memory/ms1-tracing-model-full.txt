################ MODEL: FIT
Method: every ledger row was decomposed as RSS = base(0.4 GiB) + in-use(arena - free) + resident-free R + mmapped M, with in-use = charged + uncharged-in-use. The uncharged-in-use series (arena - free - sum_mib) is a direct measurement of the arena-resident MS1 internals; M (apices, 168-B record vectors, mts, inten) is outside the arena and is what the ledger cannot see. Partition start/end found programmatically as the first/last row with band_arena>0 in phase start: TNBC13 61.22-81.35 s (distribution 59.5-61.2 s, detection to 81.5 s, gather to 82.5, split 82.5-85.1, toTrace loop to 105.5, ms1_trace charge at 108.2); TNBC1 92.1-110.0 s; D ctrl_first 16.57-23.06 s; D stream3 17.28-23.62 s.

In-use layer (TNBC13), model = 895 + W x f_run + P x min(1,(t-61.2)/8 s), W = 7.7 GiB (work_exp + bitsets, all bands), P = 4.7 GiB (parent payload), f_run = band_arena/46,997: measured vs model (MiB) 61.80: 8,301 / 8,947 (copies still in flight); 62.35: 8,860 / 9,114; 63.44: 9,536 / 9,596; 65.13: 10,537 / 10,589; 67.16: 11,182 / 11,147; 68.63: 11,358 / 11,689; 70.12: 11,049 / 11,708; 73.47: 10,804 / 10,902; 74.72: 10,548 / 10,420; 77.10: 6,865 / 6,716; 79.60: 5,811 / 5,755; 81.70: 5,612 / 5,595. ################ MODEL: FREE LIST
What the columns mean: free_mib = mallinfo2().fordblks summed over all arenas = bytes in free chunks (including each arena's top chunk) regardless of residency; arena_mib = system_mem of all heaps (excludes mmapped chunks, hblkhd, which the ledger does not record). A free() into glibc never changes residency; pages leave RSS only by (i) malloc_trim(0), (ii) heap_trim of a non-main arena whose top chunk exceeds the dynamic trim threshold (up to 64 MiB) - the -3.6 GiB arena / -3.2 GiB RSS step at 81.7 s on TNBC and -2.6 GiB at 23.6 s on D are exactly this, or (iii) munmap of chunks above the mmap threshold (apices, record vectors, mts: instant RSS drops such as -2,829 MiB at 105.5 s). Everything MS1 tracing frees in the arena - the 260 KB partition pieces (208 KB peaks + 52 KB IM), the <= 208 KB work_exp spectra, the ~300 B trace payloads - is below the threshold and therefore goes to bins and stays resident until a trim.

Free list at the 74.7 s peak = 28,605 MiB, two parts. (a) 13.8 GiB carried out of the distribution: at load end a malloc_trim (SX:3899, the [slab] line: RSS 60,199 -> 47,277) left 14,063 MiB of free chunks NON-resident (RSS - base - in-use = 0.4 GiB at 38.5 s, i.e. zero resident free). The distribution then freed 45.6 GiB of 8 MB + 2 MB spectrum chunks while allocating 47.0 GiB of 260 KB pieces; the arena grew only 3.0 GiB (60,575 -> 63,575), so ~44 GiB of the pieces were carved from freed chunks, and 13.8 GiB stayed free - the last-freed spectra (nobody left to reuse them) plus chunks in the wrong arena: a chunk returns to the arena that allocated it (the picker thread's), while a piece is drawn from the copying thread's arena, and with 100 threads there are ~100 arenas. By RSS arithmetic ~10.4 GiB of that 13.8 was resident at 61.2 s (the model's r = 3.4 GiB is the never-re-touched remainder of the trimmed 14.1 GiB; on D r = 3.8 of 12.9). (b) ~14.8 GiB net from the 18 bands that had already returned by 74.7 s: each returns its 979 MiB sub[b] and ~160 MiB work_exp + bitset (20.5 GiB freed in total), minus ~5.8 GiB that the still-running bands' growth re-took from those bins (trace payloads are small requests cut from freed pieces in the same arena). Part (b) is entirely resident - it was written moments before. So at the peak ~25 GiB of the 28.6 is resident (est.) and the remaining unattributed 39,215 = that 25 + 10.5 uncharged in-use + ~3.1 mmapped + 0.4 base.

Why RSS keeps rising to 74.7 s although in-use peaks at ~65 s (55.6 GiB): frees add to R one-for-one, while the running bands' growth (arena +5.0 GiB from 61.2 to 68.6 s despite >= 11.3 GiB free at all times) lands on NEW pages - the free chunks are in other threads' arenas or the wrong size class. RSS therefore only turns down once band returns stop being offset by growth (arena flat at 68,604 from 73 s), and it falls just 1 GiB from the peak to 81 s because every return stays resident; the one automatic give-back is the 81.7 s heap_trim step.

After the phase (84.4 s): 54,397 MiB free = arena 64,524 - in-use 10,127 = the whole partition (46,997) + all work_exp/bitsets (~7.7) + the 13.8 carried, minus what the split's structures re-took; ~51 GiB of it resident. It stays there (60.7 GiB free at 105 s, 59.9 at the "Released XICs" line) until the phase-end trim at 132 s, which returned 60,790 -> 3,762 MB, i.e. 57.0 of 59.9 GiB (95%) in 4.9 s (D: 17.6 of 20.7, 85%, 2.0 s).

Would one trim return them: yes. malloc_trim(0) in glibc >= 2.8 walks every arena's bins and MADV_DONTNEEDs the page-aligned interior of every free chunk, so fragmentation costs only the partial pages at chunk ends (<= 8 KiB per free chunk; ~230 k pieces -> <= 1.8 GiB worst case, and the measured 95% return says far less). Would the next bands re-touch them: for a trim placed right after the distribution (the running SPEXTRACTOR_MS1_TRIM arm) the ceiling is the resident part of the carried 13.8 GiB (~10.4 GiB est.), not the 28.6 at the peak - part (b) does not exist yet at that instant and is re-created resident by every band return whatever the trim did. The allocations that follow the trim are the 48 work_exp copies (~7.7 GiB of <= 208 KB pieces), the apices (mmapped, unaffected) and 4.7 GiB of ~300 B payload chunks over 20 s; glibc serves each from the requesting thread's own bins before extending its heap, so they re-touch trimmed chunks only inside the 48 band-threads' arenas. Today's run shows most of that growth did NOT find usable free chunks (arena +5.0 GiB during detection with >= 11.3 GiB always free), so the re-touch is expected to be partial: estimated effect at the 74.7 s instant -6 to -10 GiB, after which the process peak would move to ~62-65 s (all 48 bands live, in-use maximal: ~0.4 + 55.6 + M ~ 57-59 GiB, est.), i.e. back below the LOAD row (64.2) - on TNBC the load would then be the process peak again. This is a prediction for the running arm, not a result. A trim between band returns would address part (b), but that is a different placement from the one being measured.

################ DESIGN: RANKING
The phase is bound by three things in this order: the MS1 map's size (45.6 GiB = 20 B x 2.392e9 picked centroids, of which the model estimates only ~17% are ever read by MassTraceDetection), the copy of it that the band partition makes (47.0 GiB), and glibc keeping every freed chunk resident (28.6 GiB of free list at the 74.7-s peak; 54.4 GiB at POST). Ranked by GiB per unit of risk and cost:

1. (9b) prune MS1 centroids <= trace:noise_threshold_int at flush - D-class, ~50 lines, no OpenMS change. It attacks the root (the map) and everything proportional to it: est. ms1_map 45.6 -> ~7.6 GiB, partition 47.0 -> ~7.9, carried free list 28.6 -> <= 7.9; pass 1 falls to ~30-38k MiB and the WINDOW LOOP (51,738) binds -> run peak 70.2 -> ~52 GB (est.). It removes nothing the tracer can see (MTD.cpp:243), so it is not a faint-signal change, and it makes a lower-threshold sweep cheaper, not dearer. Wait for the running HIST arm to size it (every number scales with the <100 share), take the band-edge sample before the prune (else the 1e-4 edge effect the 48-band gate showed), and gate by digest. Bonus: the per-spectrum sort moves into the parallel flush and the 16-s serial sortSpectra at :3909 goes away.
2. (10) per-tile loop trim - D, ~10 lines, +10-15 s: only needed once (9b) has moved the peak to the loop; est. loop 51.7k -> 20-25k, after which pass 1 (~30-38k) binds again.

On today's code the process peak then becomes LOAD 64,186; after (9b) they trim a phase that no longer binds but keep pass 1 at ~28-30k instead of ~33-38k.
