# Bounded-parents design panel -- judge's synthesis (workflow wf_b2a2ec60, 3 designs x 3 adversarial lenses)

## Ranking
- 1. Cell-atomic grid (synthesis: design 1's verifier fix 4 + design 3's stamped-geometry reference + a frozen window frame table) -- exact, zero residual, no detector or OpenMS change; the recommendation
- 2. detector-cap (design 1 as submitted) -- the only submitted design that attacks both cause 2 and cause 3 at the root; mechanism verified; its 'geometric bound' and its science pricing are wrong
- 3. local-split (design 2) -- sound and fixable for cause 2 (EPD), leaves cause 3 untouched, whose size is unmeasured and after probe2 may be the larger half; keep as an S-gated splitter option only
- 4. priced-nonequivalence (design 3) -- refuted by probe2 (99.9% of spectra still differ under the fixed window, so the edge-open stamp would cover ~all spectra) and by the unflagged-root counterexample; salvage its instrumentation and its 'one-tile mode is the identity arm, geometry in the header' framing

## Recommendation
THE DESIGN. Detection runs per fixed RT CELL, in BOTH modes: cells of pitch P (default 600 s, `tile:rt_sec`; -1 = one cell = today's output, the identity arm) anchored on the run's MS1 frame times from the metadata frame table (n = round(P / cycle) frames per cell, last cell absorbs a remainder < P/2; the boundary LIST is stamped as `spx:tile_boundaries`). A tile is a union of whole cells (`tile:cells_per_tile`, the memory knob -- output-neutral by construction, so MemAvailable auto-sizing is allowed for it). NO detection halo: a walk that reaches a cell edge stops there in both modes, exactly as it stops at the run's edge today (:1189 exit condition). Scoring needs fragments within gate:delta_rt of a precursor, so tile k carries over from cell k-1 the children with rtOf >= T_k - 2*delta_rt (a few traces per window) and scores the precursors with rt in [T_k - delta_rt, T_{k+1} - delta_rt) (the plan's `pidx`, shifted by delta_rt); one-tile mode is the same code with every cell resident. Pass 1 (MS1) uses the same cells: MassTraceDetection per cell, EPD, trimToSpan, absorb into the global store, then FWHM/sort/inferPrecursors_/im-veto whole-run over the identical concatenated set. The detector (detectTracesInteger_ :1098-1254), splitIntegerTraces (:1285-1372), EPD, trimToSpan (:436-471) and scoreCandidates_ (:2815-2925) are byte-untouched.

IMPLEMENTATION ORDER (src/spextractor.cpp at HEAD 2fe3a23 = a70a4d0 for src; each step D-gated before the next):
1. Frozen scoring support (D-class, do it first, on today's build): the window's TraceStore frame table comes from the metadata frame table of the whole run (all frames of the window's group), not the resident slab: `wst.setFrames(...)` at :4104 takes the full table; `buildFragStats` (:1670-1710) sets `used[f] = 1` for every window frame (assert `G_live == F` per cell, warn otherwise -- measured true on D and TNBC, BASELINE 0d) and `G_override = F`; `pdense` (:4236) is sized F. Traces detected on a cell slab are rebased once (`t.frame0` via `rt_index` values, plan C5, not an offset). This makes `nearest_local` (:1682-1691) a pure function of the two frame tables and closes the MS1-XIC truncation channel (scoreCandidates_ :2827-2832) at ANY halo, including zero -- no MS1 cap, no halo sized from MS1 spans. 7. Optional, later, S-gated, not needed for invariance: the seed cap (design 1, ~25 lines, identity at 0) if bounded background parents are wanted for their own sake; LPS (design 2, with rt_idx := a) as a `trace:split_mode` science option.

THE BOUND. Exact: for any P, any partition of cells into tiles, any thread count (at the existing fixed-thread determinism), the output is bit-identical to the one-tile run at the same P, with no probabilistic argument -- every parent, child, precursor and spectrum is a deterministic function of one cell's peaks, the frozen run-level scalars (band edges, F, frame spacing, noise), the whole-run MS1 trace and the children within delta_rt of the precursor, all of which are resident in the scoring tile. Relative to the TRUE whole run (P = infinity) the output differs exactly through parents that would have crossed a grid line -- K-1 lines (12 on TNBC at 600 s, 2 on D at 600 / 5 at 300): an isolated peptide fragment or precursor is cut iff its elution crosses a line (probability ~ base width / P, ~2.5% of peptides at 15-s widths; the apex side keeps the apex, the far side becomes a duplicate flank spectrum; MS1 isotope lanes are cut at the SAME frame so xicCorr and charge inference are not disturbed, unlike a seed cap); background lanes are cut at every line and EPD/detection re-segment them from the cut along the lane within the cell -- the same churn probe2 measured (99.9% of spectra, ~half the fragment union), which becomes the fixed, reproducible reference instead of a residual. Remaining pre-existing residuals, tile-independent: per-band double claims (0.1%), equal-key fragment ties at the top-500 cap (output-invisible unless equal (c*inten, m/z) with different profiles; the plan's duplicate-key detector).

THE GATE. G0 identity: P=-1 reproduces `bc3c5ac9` (trace arm) / the TNBC pin, e2e 14/14. G1 exactness on real data: D at P=300 in 1/2/3 tiles x threads {8,100} all `SPECTRUM DATA IDENTICAL`; the two fixtures; failure detectors per cell (G_live == F, no `nearest_local == -1` for any scored precursor point, no owned trace outside its cell). G2 the science price (grid vs whole, same build, `trace:split_scan_time` fixed): Sage rank-1 q <= 0.01 on D at P in {300, 600, 1200} vs P=-1 and TNBC 009 at 600, accept count >= -0.5% and entrapment FDR <= base + 0.3 points, STRATIFIED by whether the peptide's precursor RT lies within 15 s of a grid line (the loss, if any, must live in that stratum; a uniform loss means a run-global scalar is still unfrozen) and by charge (z=1 separately); MSFragger on D at the same time (BASELINE:669 shows the engines respond oppositely to spectrum-membership churn). G3 memory/CPU: TNBC t6 as one 600-s cell: loop peak expected ~15-21 GB (probe: 29.8 with H=123, 26.7 with H=63) and detector CPU back to the whole-run share. G4 pass 1 per cell: trace/precursor symmetric difference reported, Sage. G5 release: both engines on D and A at the final P, entrapment, re-pin with P and the boundary list in the pin's name.

## Fatal findings
detector-cap, invariance (material, I agree in mechanism): on a lane whose intensity trend beats frame-to-frame noise, seeds are processed in frame order, each claims exactly [f, f+36] (the down side is already marked -> `taken` misses -> terminates, :1165/:1222), and the 37-frame lattice is phased from the lane's first free frame -- different in tile and whole unless the offset is 0 mod 37, so every segment differs for the length of the trend; no bound exists. Realism: at typical intensities noise dominates the trend, so S-maxima are random and the tail is geometric (with the verifier's correction: firewall = higher than every lane peak within 2S on the edge side, ~1/3 per S-max, not 1/2 per 2S); but 'usually geometric' is not a bound, and the taint-bit fix only measures it. Its point (3) -- core precursors' XIC points beyond the tile map to nearest_local = -1 -- is real (:1682-1691, :2831) but the fix is to freeze the frame table, not to cap MS1. The science verifier's pricing correction stands: the 0.27% child-extent figure is a LOWER bound on touched children; the cap re-segments every connected lane (common low-mass ions, contaminants) at seed +- 60 s, straddled peaks split into an apex child and a flank remnant, sign negative by mechanism; and probe2's 46% fragment disagreement says a large share of emitted fragments live on such lanes, so this science change is broader than its author priced. Verdict: the mechanism survives, the 'geometric bound' and the price do not.
local-split, invariance (material, I agree, fixable): rt_idx = smoothed argmax over [lo, hi] is not the decided maximum a; on a monotone rise there is no Step-1 maximum (k is a maximum iff s[k] >= s over [k-2, k+2), :150-195), so a keeps apex status while its cap window contains taller slope points and the reported RT lands up to W/2 from a -- the +-(W + 6 pts) window is then anchored at the wrong point and the design's own H=180 detector reports 0 violations while the child differs. One-line fix (rt_idx := a, H += delta_rt). The deeper limit is not a refutation but a scope: LPS cannot touch cause 3, and probe2 shows causes 2+3 together are 99.9% of spectra with cause 3's share unmeasured; the feasibility verifier's point that per-child IM changes every child's record at the ulp level (walk-order iwm with the seed counted twice vs frame-order mean) is correct and means 'bulk bit-identical to today' is false even where profiles are.
priced-nonequivalence, invariance (material, I agree): a whole-run parent seeded OUTSIDE the tile is replaced in the tile by a re-seed at the strongest orphan on its path, whose narrower sd (sdRobust :1121-1131 seeded from a smaller weight) terminates on outliers INSIDE the tile on both sides -> valid, no open bit, and the later edge-reaching remnants see its peaks as `taken` and are its dependants, not its roots; its spectrum differs unstamped at any depth. Science (material, now CONFIRMED by probe2): under the fixed window 99.9% of core spectra still differ at H=123; with bits inherited at :1355 the flag would mark ~all spectra, gate (3)'s S_open <= 2% fails at every halo because the parents are run-long. The MS1 flag on ms1_traces (EPD children) instead of MTD parents misses every interior child. Feasibility: run-total header stamps need the placeholder pattern; the synth fixture's amp floor (test :92) makes 'digest(T=1)==digest(T=2) with 0 open parents' unsatisfiable; edge bits set at the run's own ends in one-tile mode. Verdict: the priced-bound deliverable is dead; the instrumentation is worth keeping.

## What each design got right
detector-cap: that bounded parents are the only route to a LOCAL dependency, and that with the parent inside the tile EPD's global walk becomes harmless (verified: detectPeaks is per trace, :1340; the chunking cannot change per-trace output); the cap placed before the frame is scanned so scans/missed/msr stay local and S=0 is byte-identical; the halo derivation H = 2S + delta_rt given a cap; that the MS1 path needs the same treatment IF MS1 is capped (MTD's isTraceValid_ is whole-trace, MassTraceDetection.cpp:444-468); the parent-extents histogram that nobody has (hoisting `extents` above :4153) -- kept in the recommendation. local-split: the correct anatomy of EPD -- smoothing and Step-1 maxima are local (ElutionPeakDetection.cpp:150-208), only the Step-2 retained-maximum walk (:213-307) is parent-global; the pairwise-consistent partition proof (nearest separated partner's argmin coincides from both sides); that children inherit the parent's IM (:1339 -> EPD :500) and that this is its own boundary channel; the honest statement that detection membership is the residual. priced-nonequivalence: that the tiled run at a stamped geometry must be THE reference and one-tile mode its identity arm (adopted); that a halo buys insulation, never exactness; the correct rejection of the seed-owned on-demand-fetch hybrid (it must re-run the window's detection and converges to the whole run); the edge-open/cut bit, the `lo_is_cut/hi_is_cut` bools, per-spectrum stamps and the specdiff classification (adopted as instrumentation); the observation that flat background children score 0.5-0.9 under zero-padded Pearson (its science verifier's correction) which explains why ~half the fragment union is on long parents. The verifiers, collectively: the MS1-XIC truncation channel (:1682-1691 -> :2831) that every design missed and that a frozen frame table closes for free; the synth fixture's amp floor making every trace run-long; the `in.size() < 2` guard at :1287; the unstable sort at :4212 as pre-existing.

## Open questions
1. How much of probe2's 53% fragment disagreement is detection (cause 3) vs EPD (cause 2)? Experiment: rerun the t6 probe (SPEXTRACTOR_MS2_RT_RANGE=3600,4200,123, frozen edges/G, whole-run MS1) and the whole run both with `-trace:ms2_split_valleys 0` (:1287 then returns parents unsplit), dump per-window parent TSVs (mz, rt, im, intensity, npts, len, first/last RT -- plan C6 tooling), and take the symmetric difference restricted to parents with rt in the core: that is cause 3 alone; probe2 minus it is cause 2. Decides whether LPS would ever have sufficed (it changes nothing for the grid, which is exact either way, but it prices what the halo designs were fighting and whether the seed cap is worth its science change later).
2. The science price of the grid as a function of P. Experiment: the same build at `tile:rt_sec` in {-1, 300, 600, 1200} on D and {-1, 600} on TNBC 009, `trace:split_scan_time` fixed, Sage + entrapment, MSFragger on D via bench/fix_mzml_cvparams.py; report count, set overlap, and the loss stratified by |precursor RT - nearest grid line| <= 15 s vs > 15 s, and z=1 separately. Prediction: count within +-0.5%, set churn 5-7% (probe1 gave +0.26% / 93.6% overlap for cutting at the same lines with a halo), loss concentrated in the straddle stratum. If the straddle stratum carries > 0.5% loss, the next step is the partition-cut refinement: per (tof bin, IM bin) a cut frame = argmin of summed intensity over a +-30 s zone around the line, a pure function of the raw zone peaks, with the walk unable to accept peaks assigned to the other side -- still exact by the same partition argument, cuts land in valleys, needs a raw-peak halo of 30 s used only for the cut table (~80 lines, gate it only if this experiment demands it).
3. Does G_live == F hold on every CELL (a quiet cell of a sparse window may have a frame with no fragment point)? Measured only per whole window (24/24 on D). Experiment: the per-cell `[gstat]` assertion in step 1 on D at P=300 and TNBC at 600. If it fails somewhere, `used` frozen to all frames is still tile-independent but differs from today's nearest_local on those frames -- the P=-1 digest check (G0) shows whether that is observable.
4. Memory and wall without a halo. Experiment: TNBC t6 as one 600-s cell vs the probe's H=63/123 rows (26.7/29.8 GB, 2,352/2,682 CPU-s); the H=63 row already brackets it from above. Also the reader's own halo when `trace:native_ms2_neighbors > 0` (raw frames only, does not touch detection).
5. Pending and cheap: the 10-ppm-tolerant specdiff on probe2 (bench/mzml_specdiff.py at HEAD) tells whether the 46.4M/46.2M one-sided fragments are apex-frame shifts of the same fragments or different traces; it changes how the grid's churn vs the true whole run should be read, not the design.

## Decision for the user
Three options. (A, recommended) Cell-atomic grid: run detection per fixed 600-s RT cell anchored at the run start, in tiled AND one-tile mode, with no detection halo, a delta_rt carry-over of boundary children for scoring, and the window's frame table frozen from metadata; the detector, EPD and scoring code are untouched, ~200 lines inside the plan's existing reader/writer work, no OpenMS patch. It is the only option with an exact guarantee -- bit-identical output for any tiling of the cells, checkable on real data (dataset D at P=300 in 1/2/3 tiles must digest-match) -- and it has the lowest memory (no halo: ~15-21 GB per 600-s tile vs the probe's 21-30). Its cost is a science change confined to the 12 cut lines: ~2.5% of peptides straddle a line (apex side keeps its apex, far side becomes a duplicate flank spectrum, isotope lanes cut symmetrically), plus background-lane re-segmentation within each cell -- the same churn probe2 already measured (99.9% of spectra, about half the fragment union), which becomes the fixed reference rather than a residual; gate it grid-600 vs whole with Sage/MSFragger/entrapment, stratified by distance to the lines (expected within +-0.5%; if the straddle stratum shows loss, the valley-seeking partition cut is the next step and stays exact). (B) The seed cap (design 1) with a taint bit: locality without a fixed grid, keeps the 123-s halo, residual on gap-free lanes measured rather than bounded, and a broader science change (every connected lane re-segmented at +-60 s, negative by mechanism); ~25 lines plus an MS1 patch. (C) LPS (design 2): fixes EPD only; after probe2 the unmeasured detection channel may be the larger half, so it cannot deliver invariance alone; worth keeping only as a splitter science option. Take A; before writing code, run the two zero-code measurements (the `ms2_split_valleys=0` probe to split cause 2 from 3, and the 10-ppm specdiff) so the science price of the cut is understood, then step 1 (frozen frame table, D-class) on today's build.
